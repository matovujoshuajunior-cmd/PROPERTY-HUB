About Property Hub Uganda

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Property Hub exists for one reason: renting and buying property in Uganda shouldn't feel like a gamble.

The problem we solve

Every day, thousands of Ugandans and diaspora families lose money to fake listings, misleading prices, and brokers who vanish after taking a deposit. The traditional broker model has no accountability and no way to check who you're dealing with.

What we built

Property Hub fixes this in three ways:

  • Compulsory verification — every broker pays a 5,000 UGX verification fee and submits landlord contacts, title documents, and photos before a property can appear as "Verified."
  • Public broker reviews — visitors rate brokers after every interaction. Brokers with 4★+ and 10+ reviews earn a Trusted badge.
  • Lead accountability — visitors fill a short quiz before contacting a broker, so brokers get serious leads, not time-wasters.

Who we serve

  • Locals looking for single rooms in Kikoni, apartments in Naalya, or land in Kisaasi
  • Diaspora buyers in the US, UK, and UAE investing in Uganda remotely
  • Brokers who want to be found by serious buyers and stand out from scammers

Our team

We are a small team based in Mulago, Kampala. You can reach us at hello@propertyhub.dpdns.org or on WhatsApp at +256 700 000 000.

Our promise

If you ever lose money because of a property you found on Property Hub after following our verification steps, we will help you report it to the police and connect you with a lawyer. We can't promise to refund you — but we can promise not to hide behind a website.
