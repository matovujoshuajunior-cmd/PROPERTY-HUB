1. What Property Hub is

Property Hub is a listing platform that connects property seekers with brokers and property owners. We do not own, rent, or manage any property listed on the site.

2. What Property Hub is not

We are not a real estate agency, legal advisor, or party to any transaction between you and a broker. All negotiations, payments, and agreements are strictly between you and the broker.

3. Verification

When a listing shows "Verified," our team has reviewed the property details and a fee has been paid by the broker. This does not guarantee the property is still available, nor does it remove the need for you to view the property in person.

4. Never pay before viewing

Never transfer money before viewing a property in person. If a broker asks for a "viewing fee," report them immediately via the Report button on their profile.

5. Broker obligations

Brokers agree to: (a) list only properties they have permission to advertise, (b) provide accurate photos and prices, (c) submit truthful verification documents, (d) not harass or defraud visitors.

6. Fees

Verification and lead unlock fees are deducted from the broker's wallet. Fees are non-refundable once a lead is unlocked or a verification is approved. See the Pricing tab in your dashboard for current rates.

7. Limitation of liability

Property Hub is not responsible for any loss, damage, or dispute arising from a transaction between you and a broker. Use common sense — verify titles, meet in person, and never sign anything you don't understand.
