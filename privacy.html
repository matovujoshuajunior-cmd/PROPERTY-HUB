Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What we collect

  • Visitors: name, email, WhatsApp number when you submit a "Connect" request
  • Brokers: business name, email, phone, password (hashed), verification documents
  • Technical: browser, device type, IP address — for security only

How we use it

  • To notify the broker when you request a property
  • To notify you when the broker approves your request
  • To send a follow-up email 3 days later asking how it went
  • To prevent fraud and abuse

Who we share it with

We share your contact details only with the specific broker of the property you requested. We do not sell your data. We use:

  • Supabase (database & authentication) — EU/US servers
  • Web3Forms (transactional email delivery)
  • Selected partners — only when you click a link in a follow-up email

Your rights

You can request deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing privacy@propertyhub.dpdns.org. We respond within 14 days.

Cookies

We use a single functional cookie to keep brokers logged in to their dashboard. No advertising cookies.
