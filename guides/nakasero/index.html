Apartments for Rent in Nakasero

Last updated: November 2026

Nakasero is Kampala's administrative and diplomatic heart — home to ministries, embassies, and the city's most prestigious addresses. Apartments here are modern, secure, and expensive. If you work in the city centre or want to be at the centre of everything, this is where you look.

Why rent in Nakasero

  • Walk to work — ministries, banks, and embassies are all within walking distance.
  • Top restaurants and hotels — Kampala Serena, Sheraton, and the best dining in the city.
  • Very secure — diplomatic-level security is standard.
  • Modern apartments — pools, gyms, and backup power are common in newer buildings [citation:15][citation:22].
  • Central location — 5 minutes to Kampala Road, 10 to Kololo, 15 to Nakawa.

Typical prices in 2026

  • 1-bedroom apartment: 1,500,000 – 2,500,000 UGX/month
  • 2-bedroom apartment: 2,500,000 – 4,000,000 UGX/month
  • 3-bedroom luxury apartment (pool/gym): 5,000,000 – 8,000,000 UGX/month
  • Furnished serviced apartment: 6,000,000+ UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Nakasero

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an apartment for rent in Nakasero cost in 2026?

Nakasero is one of Kampala's most expensive areas. 1-bedroom apartments start at 1,500,000 UGX/month. 2-bedroom units range from 2,500,000 to 4,000,000 UGX. 3-bedroom luxury apartments with pools and gyms cost 5,000,000+ UGX.

Is Nakasero good for business travellers?

Yes. Nakasero is the heart of Kampala's business district. It is walking distance to ministries, banks, embassies, and top hotels.

What is Nakasero known for?

Nakasero is Kampala's administrative and diplomatic centre. It is home to the President's office, the Ministry of Finance, numerous embassies, and upscale hotels.
