What to expect in Kikoni

  • 5-minute walk to Makerere main campus
  • Buzzing nightlife, cheap food, student services everywhere
  • Water and electricity are usually prepaid
  • Street parking is scarce — plan to walk or use boda-boda

Typical prices in 2026

  • Bare room, shared bathroom: 100,000 – 150,000 UGX/month
  • Room with private bathroom: 180,000 – 250,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained room: 300,000+ UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Kikoni

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Kikoni safe?

Kikoni is generally safe during the day. At night, stick to lit streets and avoid walking alone. Choose listings with locked gates.

How much deposit should I pay in Kikoni?

Standard is 1 month deposit + 1 month rent upfront. Anything more is suspicious.
