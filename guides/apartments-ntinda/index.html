Apartments for Rent in Ntinda

Last updated: October 2026

Ntinda is a vibrant commercial and residential area in northeast Kampala. It has everything a young professional needs — shopping, restaurants, banks, and good transport — within walking distance.

Why live in Ntinda

  • Walking distance to Ntinda Shopping Complex and banks
  • Strong transport links to Kampala Road, Kisaasi, and Naalya
  • Range of options from budget studios to luxury 3-bedrooms

Typical prices in 2026

  • Studio / 1 bedroom: 700,000 – 1,200,000 UGX/month
  • 2 bedroom: 1,200,000 – 2,000,000 UGX/month
  • 3 bedroom: 2,000,000 – 3,500,000 UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Ntinda

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.
