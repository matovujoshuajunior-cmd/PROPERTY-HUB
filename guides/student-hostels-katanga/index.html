About Katanga

Katanga sits between Makerere's main campus and the Mulago hospital complex. It grew organically in the 1970s and 80s as the first wave of Makerere students spilled out of the university's limited hostels. Today it's a dense, mostly single-storey neighborhood — narrow lanes, small compounds, and a mix of purpose-built hostels and rented rooms in family homes.

Because Katanga is older, its buildings are more worn than Kikoni's or Wandegeya's. But that's also why it's cheap. If you're willing to accept a shared bathroom and occasional water cuts, you'll pay 30–40% less than Wandegeya for the same walking distance to campus.

Safety and sanitation in Katanga

Katanga has a reputation for being rough, but the reality is more balanced. It's a student neighborhood — the main risks are petty theft and noise, not violent crime. To stay safe:

  • Choose a gated hostel with a guard. Many Katanga hostels have this; some don't. Ask directly.
  • Visit at night once before signing. Check lighting on the access road and noise levels in the compound.
  • Don't leave windows open when you go out — ground-floor rooms are easiest to break into.
  • Ask about water storage. Buildings with rooftop tanks keep flowing during National Water outages.
  • Check shared bathrooms at peak hours (7 AM). If they're flooded or dirty now, they'll be worse at 6 AM.
  • Ask for the LC1 chairperson's contact. Every legitimate hostel can provide this.

How to avoid scams in Katanga

Every intake season, fake brokers post photos of rooms in Katanga that don't exist. Follow these rules:

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions.
  2. Meet at the property itself. Not at a nearby shop, not at the hostel office — inside the room you're renting.
  3. Prefer Verified listings on Property Hub Uganda — checked by our team before going live.
  4. Check the broker's reviews and Trusted badge. A Trusted broker has 4★+ rating from 10+ reviews.
  5. Get a written receipt. Even a handwritten one with the landlord's name, phone, and signature protects you.
  6. Never pay a "viewing fee." Legitimate brokers are paid by the landlord.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much are student hostels in Katanga in 2026?

Bare hostel rooms start at 120,000 UGX/month. Rooms with private bathrooms cost 200,000–300,000. Serviced hostels with utilities and security range from 300,000 to 500,000 UGX.

Is Katanga safe for students?

Katanga is a busy student neighborhood and generally safe during the day. At night, stick to the main roads and avoid narrow alleys. Choose a gated hostel with a guard and read reviews before paying.

What is the difference between Katanga and Kikoni?

Katanga is smaller, slightly cheaper, and older. Kikoni is larger, has more hostels and food spots, and better road access. Katanga suits students on the tightest budget. Kikoni suits those who want variety and easier transport.

How far is Katanga from Makerere University?

Katanga is a 10–20 minute walk from Makerere's main campus, depending on where in Katanga you stay. Most students walk daily or take a boda for 1,000–2,000 UGX.

What should I check before renting a hostel in Katanga?

Check water pressure, bathroom condition, power supply, and whether the compound has a guard. Visit at night once to check noise and lighting. Ask the LC1 chairperson's contact if unsure about the landlord.
