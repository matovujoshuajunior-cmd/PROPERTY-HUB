Student Hostels in Katanga

Last updated: October 2026

Katanga is the oldest and cheapest student ghetto near Makerere. If you are on a tight budget and want a social environment, Katanga is the move — but you must know what you are getting into.

Verified Student Hostels in Katanga

These listings are updated in real time. Every broker is rated, and you can contact them directly.

What to expect in Katanga

  • Cheapest rooms near Makerere (from 120,000 UGX)
  • Very social, tight-knit community
  • Shared bathrooms and prepaid water are the norm
  • 10-minute walk to campus

Typical prices in 2026

  • Bare room, shared bathroom: 120,000 – 180,000 UGX/month
  • Room with shared kitchen: 180,000 – 250,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained: 300,000+ UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Katanga

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Katanga cheaper than Kikoni?

Slightly — Katanga is the cheapest of the Makerere ghettos. But it is also the most crowded.

Do I need a deposit in Katanga?

Yes, usually 1 month. Always get a written receipt with the landlord's name and phone.
