Houses for Rent in Kyebando

Last updated: November 2026

Kyebando is one of Kampala's quieter residential neighborhoods, tucked between Kisaasi and Ntinda. It has fewer apartment blocks and more standalone homes, making it ideal for families and professionals who want space and privacy without leaving the city.

Why rent in Kyebando

  • Quiet residential streets — less traffic and noise than Kisaasi or Ntinda.
  • More space — standalone homes with compounds are common here.
  • Close to Kisaasi and Ntinda — shopping and transport hubs are 10 minutes away.
  • Growing area — new developments are appearing, especially serviced apartments and Airbnbs [citation:2][citation:30].
  • Good for families — schools and clinics are nearby.

Typical prices in 2026

  • Single room, shared bathroom: 120,000 – 200,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained single room: 250,000 – 400,000 UGX/month
  • 1-bedroom apartment: 350,000 – 550,000 UGX/month
  • 2-bedroom house with compound: 600,000 – 1,000,000 UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Kyebando

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a house for rent in Kyebando cost in 2026?

Single rooms start at 120,000 UGX/month. Self-contained rooms range from 250,000 to 400,000 UGX. One-bedroom apartments cost 350,000 to 550,000 UGX.

Is Kyebando a quiet area?

Yes. Kyebando is more residential and quieter than Kisaasi or Ntinda. It is popular with families and professionals who want to avoid main road noise.

How far is Kyebando from Kampala city centre?

Kyebando is about 6–7 km from Kampala Road. By boda, it takes 15–20 minutes depending on traffic.
