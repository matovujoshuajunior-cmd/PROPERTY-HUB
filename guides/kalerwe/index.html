Cheap Houses for Rent in Kalerwe

Last updated: November 2026

Kalerwe is the budget capital of northern Kampala. Rooms here start at 80,000 UGX/month — cheaper than anywhere else within walking distance of Mulago Hospital and Makerere University. The trade-off is space and quiet, but for students and hospital staff on tight budgets, it is unbeatable.

Why rent in Kalerwe

  • Cheapest rooms in Kampala — single rooms from 80,000 UGX.
  • Kalerwe Market — fresh produce at wholesale prices, right on your doorstep [citation:18].
  • Walk to Mulago Hospital — 15 minutes on foot, popular with nurses and medical staff.
  • Close to Makerere — 20-minute walk or 5-minute boda to campus.
  • Local transport — taxis and bodas run frequently to the city centre.

Typical prices in 2026

  • Bare single room, shared bathroom: 80,000 – 150,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained single room: 150,000 – 250,000 UGX/month
  • 1-bedroom apartment: 250,000 – 400,000 UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Kalerwe

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a room for rent in Kalerwe cost in 2026?

Kalerwe is one of Kampala's cheapest areas. Single rooms start at 80,000 UGX/month. Self-contained rooms range from 150,000 to 250,000 UGX.

Is Kalerwe safe?

Kalerwe is busy and crowded, especially around the market. It is generally safe during the day. At night, stick to well-lit streets and avoid walking alone.

What is Kalerwe known for?

Kalerwe is famous for its fresh produce market. It is also close to Mulago Hospital and Makerere University.
