Houses for Rent in Kololo

Last updated: November 2026

Kololo is Kampala's premier residential hill — quiet, green, and home to embassies, expats, and senior professionals. If you want space, security, and views, this is where you look. The prices reflect it.

Why rent in Kololo

  • Quiet, leafy streets — one of the calmest areas in Kampala.
  • Expat and diplomatic hub — embassies, international schools, and high-end restaurants [citation:14].
  • Walk to Acacia Mall — 15 minutes on foot to shopping and dining.
  • Hilltop views — many homes have views over Kampala.
  • Very secure — gated compounds and 24/7 guards are standard.

Typical prices in 2026

  • 1-bedroom apartment: 1,500,000 – 2,500,000 UGX/month
  • 2-bedroom apartment: 2,500,000 – 4,000,000 UGX/month
  • 3-bedroom house: 3,500,000 – 6,000,000 UGX/month
  • 4+ bedroom furnished house: 6,000,000 – 10,000,000+ UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Kololo

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a house for rent in Kololo cost in 2026?

Kololo is one of Kampala's most expensive areas. Apartments start at 1,500,000 UGX/month. Standalone houses range from 2,500,000 to 6,000,000 UGX. Furnished homes can exceed 8,000,000 UGX.

Is Kololo good for expats?

Yes. Kololo is one of the top expat neighborhoods in Kampala, along with Naguru and Muyenga. It has quiet streets, international schools, embassies, and upscale restaurants.

What is Kololo known for?

Kololo is known for its hilltop views, tree-lined streets, and proximity to Acacia Mall and Kisementi. It is home to embassies, senior government officials, and expatriates.
