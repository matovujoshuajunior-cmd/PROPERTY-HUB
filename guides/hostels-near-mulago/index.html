Who stays in hostels near Mulago

Unlike other Kampala neighborhoods, Mulago's rental market serves three very different groups:

  • Medical students at Makerere's College of Health Sciences — need long-term rooms close to lectures and wards.
  • Interns and nurses on hospital rotations — need short-term (3–12 months) furnished rooms with reliable power and water.
  • Patient families from upcountry — need daily or weekly rates for 1–4 weeks while a relative is admitted.

Because of this mix, hostels near Mulago tend to be cleaner and better maintained than student housing in Kikoni. Landlords know their tenants are professionals who'll complain if things break.

Where the hostels are

  • Mulago Hill — the closest option. Hostels here are within 5 minutes of the hospital main gate. Premium pricing: 350,000–700,000 UGX/month.
  • Upper Kikoni (near Mulago side) — 10 minutes walking. More student-focused, cheaper: 250,000–450,000 UGX.
  • Katanga (Mulago side) — 15 minutes walking. Cheapest of the three: 200,000–350,000 UGX.
  • Wandegeya — 20 minutes walking or 5 by boda. Modern hostels and furnished apartments: 400,000–800,000 UGX.

Safety checklist for hostels near Mulago

  1. Gated compound with 24-hour guard. Non-negotiable if you work night shifts.
  2. Female-only floor or block if you're a female student or nurse. Many hostels offer this — ask.
  3. Working fire extinguisher and emergency exit. Especially in high-rise hostels on Mulago Hill.
  4. Clean shared bathrooms. Inspect at 7 AM, not during the slow afternoon.
  5. Water storage tank on the roof. National Water can be unreliable — hostels with their own tank keep flowing during outages.
  6. Read reviews. On Property Hub Uganda, check the broker's rating and Trusted badge before paying.
⚠️ For patient families: many guesthouses near Mulago offer discounted weekly rates if you book 7+ nights. Ask directly — the discount isn't usually advertised.

When to book

Medical student intakes align with Makerere's academic calendar (August and January), so hostel demand peaks in July and December. Interns and nurses rotate throughout the year. Patient family demand is unpredictable — if you need a room near the hospital urgently, call ahead and reserve by phone before travelling.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do hostels near Mulago Hospital cost?

Between 200,000 and 700,000 UGX per month. Basic hostel rooms with shared bathrooms start at 200,000. Modern hostels with private bathrooms, WiFi, and security range from 400,000 to 700,000 UGX.

Are there hostels near Mulago for patient families?

Yes. Several guesthouses and short-stay hostels near Mulago accept patient families on daily or weekly rates, typically 30,000–80,000 UGX per night depending on room type.

Which hostels are closest to Mulago Hospital?

The closest hostels are on Mulago Hill itself, followed by those in Katanga and the upper Kikoni area. All are within 10–15 minutes walking distance of the hospital main gate.

Are hostels near Mulago safe for female students and nurses?

Yes, if you choose the right one. Look for gated compounds, 24-hour security, female-only floors where available, and always read reviews. Prefer Verified listings on Property Hub Uganda.

Do hostels near Mulago include utilities?

Most modern hostels include water, electricity, and security in the monthly rent. Older or cheaper hostels may charge separately for electricity tokens. Always confirm what is included before signing.
