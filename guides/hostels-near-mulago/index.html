How to avoid rental scams near Mulago

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are hostels near Mulago safe at night?

Yes, but always choose one with a locked gate and a guard. Ask for the security arrangement before paying.

Do hostels near Mulago include food?

Some do — full board hostels include meals. Ask clearly: bed only, bed + breakfast, or full board.
