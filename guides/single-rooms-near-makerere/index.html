Where to find single rooms near Makerere

The five main student neighborhoods around Makerere are:

  • Kikoni — the biggest student ghetto. Rooms from 150,000 UGX/month. Five minutes from campus, packed with hostels and food spots.
  • Katanga — smaller, more crowded, cheaper. Rooms from 120,000 UGX. Shared bathrooms are common.
  • Wandegeya — more commercial. Single rooms 250,000–400,000 UGX with better amenities and reliable power.
  • Mulago — quieter, next to the hospital. Popular with medical students and nurses. Rooms from 200,000 UGX.
  • Kalerwe and Bwaise — cheaper but farther. 100,000–180,000 UGX. You'll need a boda daily.

How to avoid rental scams near Makerere

Every semester, students lose money to fake listings. Follow these rules:

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe, not at a parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings on Property Hub Uganda — reviewed by our team before going live.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ rating and 10+ reviews.
  5. Ask for the LC1 chairperson's contact for the area. Legitimate landlords will provide it.
  6. Sign a simple tenancy agreement. Even a handwritten one is enforceable in Uganda.
⚠️ Beware of brokers who ask for "viewing fees" before showing a room. Legitimate brokers are paid by the landlord, not by you.

When to start looking

Makerere intakes are in August and January. The best rooms go by mid-July and mid-December. Start searching 3–4 weeks before your reporting date. If you wait until orientation week, you'll pay 30–50% more for worse rooms — sometimes from a broker who knows you're desperate.

If you're on a tight budget, look in Katanga first. If you want safety and quiet, focus on Mulago. If you want modern amenities and can afford it, Wandegeya is your best bet.
