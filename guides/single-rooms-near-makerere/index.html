Where to find single rooms near Makerere

  • Kikoni — the biggest student ghetto. Rooms from 150,000 UGX/month. Five minutes from campus.
  • Katanga — smaller, more crowded, cheaper. Rooms from 120,000 UGX.
  • Wandegeya — more commercial. Single rooms 250,000–400,000 UGX with better amenities.
  • Mulago — quieter, next to the hospital. Popular with medical students. Rooms from 200,000 UGX.

Typical prices in 2026

  • Bare single room, shared bathroom: 100,000 – 180,000 UGX/month
  • Single room with private bathroom: 180,000 – 300,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained single room: 300,000 – 500,000 UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams near Makerere

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker’s reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

How much does a single room near Makerere cost in 2026?
