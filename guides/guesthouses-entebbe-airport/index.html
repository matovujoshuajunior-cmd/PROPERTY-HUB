Why stay near Entebbe Airport

  • 10–15 minutes from the terminal — no traffic risk
  • Cheaper than airport hotels
  • Good for layovers, early flights, or business trips
  • Many offer airport pickup

Typical prices in 2026

  • Budget guesthouse (shared bathroom): 80,000 – 120,000 UGX/night
  • Standard guesthouse room: 120,000 – 200,000 UGX/night

How to avoid booking scams in Entebbe

  1. Never pay before seeing the property photos and reviews.
  2. Confirm airport pickup in writing (WhatsApp screenshot).
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.
