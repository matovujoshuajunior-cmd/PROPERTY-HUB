Houses for Rent in Kanyanya

Last updated: November 2026

Kanyanya is a peaceful residential area in northern Kampala, known for the Bahai Temple and its quiet, family-friendly streets. It is a good alternative to Kisaasi for renters who want space and calm without moving too far from the city.

Why rent in Kanyanya

  • Quiet and safe — residential streets with low traffic.
  • Bahai Temple — peaceful gardens and a Kampala landmark [citation:12].
  • Family-friendly — schools, clinics, and supermarkets within walking distance.
  • Close to Kisaasi and Ntinda — 10 minutes by boda for shopping and restaurants.
  • More space for your money — standalone homes and compounds are common.

Typical prices in 2026

  • Single room, shared bathroom: 150,000 – 250,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained single room: 250,000 – 400,000 UGX/month
  • 1-bedroom apartment: 350,000 – 500,000 UGX/month
  • 2-bedroom house: 600,000 – 1,000,000 UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Kanyanya

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a house for rent in Kanyanya cost in 2026?

Single rooms start at 150,000 UGX/month. Self-contained rooms range from 250,000 to 400,000 UGX. One-bedroom apartments cost 350,000 to 500,000 UGX.

Is Kanyanya a good area for families?

Yes. Kanyanya is quiet, safe, and has good access to schools and clinics. It is popular with families who want to avoid the noise of the city centre.

What is there to do in Kanyanya?

The Bahai Temple is the main landmark, with peaceful gardens open to visitors. Kanyanya is also close to Kisaasi and Ntinda for shopping and restaurants.
