Houses for Rent in Kamwokya

Last updated: November 2026

Kamwokya sits between Kololo, Mulago, and Kisementi — one of Kampala's most connected neighborhoods. It attracts NGO workers, young professionals, and students who want to walk to Acacia Mall and still find affordable rooms. This guide shows verified listings and what to expect in 2026.

Why rent in Kamwokya

  • Walk to Acacia Mall — supermarkets, restaurants, cinemas, and banks within 10 minutes.
  • Close to Mulago Hospital — popular with medical staff and NGO workers [citation:1].
  • Central location — 10 minutes to Kampala Road, 15 to Kololo, 20 to Ntinda.
  • Range of prices — from budget single rooms to furnished apartments.
  • Active nightlife — Kisementi and Kololo nightlife is a short boda ride away.

Typical prices in 2026

  • Single room, shared bathroom: 150,000 – 250,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained single room: 300,000 – 450,000 UGX/month
  • 1-bedroom apartment: 400,000 – 700,000 UGX/month
  • 2-bedroom apartment: 700,000 – 1,200,000 UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Kamwokya

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a house for rent in Kamwokya cost in 2026?

Single rooms start at 150,000 UGX/month. One-bedroom apartments range from 400,000 to 700,000 UGX. Two-bedroom units cost 700,000 to 1,200,000 UGX.

Is Kamwokya a good area for young professionals?

Yes. Kamwokya is walking distance to Acacia Mall, Kisementi, and Kololo. It has a mix of budget rooms and mid-range apartments, making it popular with NGO workers and young professionals.

What is the best part of Kamwokya to rent?

Upper Kamwokya near Mulago Hill offers quieter streets and better views. Lower Kamwokya near the main road is busier but cheaper and closer to transport.
