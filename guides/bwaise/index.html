Cheap Rooms for Rent in Bwaise

Last updated: November 2026

Bwaise is one of Kampala's most affordable neighborhoods, popular with students and young workers who want to be close to Makerere and Mulago without paying Kikoni prices. It is busy, crowded, and sometimes floods — but the rent is hard to beat.

Why rent in Bwaise

  • Cheapest rooms near Makerere — single rooms from 100,000 UGX.
  • Close to Makerere University — 5–10 minutes by boda [citation:5].
  • Near Mulago Hospital — 10 minutes by boda.
  • Active local market — fresh food and cheap goods.
  • Good transport links — taxis and bodas run frequently to the city centre.

Typical prices in 2026

  • Bare single room, shared bathroom: 100,000 – 180,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained single room: 200,000 – 350,000 UGX/month
  • 1-bedroom apartment: 300,000 – 450,000 UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Bwaise

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a room for rent in Bwaise cost in 2026?

Bwaise is one of Kampala's most affordable areas. Single rooms start at 100,000 UGX/month. Self-contained rooms range from 200,000 to 350,000 UGX.

Is Bwaise prone to flooding?

Parts of Bwaise are low-lying and can flood during heavy rains. Check the specific street and ask neighbours before renting. Upper Bwaise and Kazo are less affected.

Is Bwaise close to Makerere University?

Yes. Bwaise is about 2–3 km from Makerere University. By boda, it takes 5–10 minutes.
