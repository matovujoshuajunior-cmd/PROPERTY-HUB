Rooms for Rent in Wandegeya

Last updated: October 2026

Wandegeya is more commercial than Kikoni or Katanga — a real Kampala suburb with supermarkets, hospitals, and modern rooms. Prices are higher, but so is the quality of living.

Why choose Wandegeya

  • Modern rooms with tiled floors and better plumbing
  • Walking distance to Makerere and Mulago
  • Good restaurants, supermarkets, and transport links
  • Safer at night than Kikoni

Typical prices in 2026

  • Single room: 250,000 – 400,000 UGX/month
  • Self-contained single room: 400,000 – 600,000 UGX/month
  • Studio apartment: 600,000 – 900,000 UGX/month

How to avoid rental scams in Wandegeya

  1. Never pay before viewing. No exceptions, no matter how good the price.
  2. Meet the landlord or broker at the property. Not at a cafe or parking lot.
  3. Prefer Verified listings — these have been reviewed by our team.
  4. Check the broker's reviews. A Trusted badge means 4★+ and 10+ reviews.
