Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes — creating an account and listing properties is 100% free. You only pay small fees when you choose to verify a listing (5,000 UGX) or unlock a lead (1,750–5,000+ UGX, deducted from your wallet).

Why do I need to list 2 properties?

A broker with 2+ listings is 4× more likely to receive serious buyer interest. It's our way of ensuring the platform has real, active inventory — which benefits everyone, especially you. Listing 2 properties takes about 10 minutes.

How do leads work?

When a visitor wants to view a property, they fill a short quiz. You get an SMS + email with their first name only. If you want their full contact, click "Unlock" in your dashboard and pay the small lead fee. Contact details stay hidden until you pay — so you never see a lead you can't act on.

What if I don't want to be verified?

Verification is compulsory for all new listings — it's how we protect visitors from scams and keep the platform trustworthy. The 5,000 UGX fee protects your reputation too: buyers trust verified brokers, and verified brokers rank higher in search.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can pause or delete any listing from your dashboard. There are no subscriptions or monthly fees — you only pay when you actively use the platform (verification or lead unlock).
