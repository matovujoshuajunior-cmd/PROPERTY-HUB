Common questions

How do brokers get leads on Property Hub Uganda?

When a visitor is interested in your listing, they fill a short qualifying quiz. You receive an SMS and email with their first name and lead score. You can unlock their full contact details (name, WhatsApp, email) by paying a small lead fee from your wallet. First broker to unlock wins the lead.

Is it really free to list properties?

Yes. Creating a broker account and listing your first 2 properties is completely free. You only pay small fees when you choose to verify a listing (5,000 UGX) or unlock a qualified lead (from 1,750 UGX). No monthly subscription, no listing fees, no hidden charges.

Why do I need to list 2 properties?

Brokers with 2 or more listings are 4× more likely to receive serious buyer interest. Requiring 2 properties ensures every broker on the platform has real, active inventory — which benefits everyone, especially you. Listing 2 properties takes about 10 minutes.

How much does a lead cost in Kampala?

Lead unlock fees range from 1,750 UGX for cold leads to about 5,000+ UGX for hot leads, based on property price. For a 500,000 UGX rental, a hot lead costs about 5,000 UGX to unlock. For guesthouses, it's 4% of the property price.

Can I pause or delete my listings anytime?

Yes. From your dashboard you can pause any listing (hides it from search without deleting it) or remove it entirely. There are no lock-in periods or cancellation fees.
