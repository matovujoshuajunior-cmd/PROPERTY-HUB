Welcome to Property Hub

Here's how to start getting leads in 5 minutes.

1

Add your first property

Click Add Property in your dashboard. Enter the title, price, type, and area. Upload 2–3 clear photos. That's it — your listing is live on the site.

2

Get verified (recommended)

Verified listings rank higher and get more clicks. Cost: 5,000 UGX from your wallet. This protects visitors from scams and builds your reputation.

3

Receive leads

When a visitor is interested in your property, they fill a short quiz. You'll be notified by SMS and email. Each lead is scored: Hot, Warm, or Cold.

4

Unlock serious leads

You decide which leads to unlock. Hot leads cost more, cold leads cost less. Unlock fee comes from your wallet. Once unlocked, you see the visitor's name, email, and WhatsApp number — and they see your contact too.

✓

Top up your wallet

Click Top Up on your dashboard to add funds via MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. Wallet pays for verifications and lead unlocks.

💡 Pro tip: Listings with 3+ photos and detailed titles like "2 Bedroom Apartment in Naalya under 800k" get 4× more views than vague titles.
