📜 Terms of Service

1. Platform Use

Property Hub Uganda is a marketplace connecting property seekers with brokers. We do not own or manage any properties listed on this platform.

2. Visitor Safety

Always verify properties in person before making any payments. Never send money to a broker without viewing the property first. Report suspicious activity immediately.

3. Broker Responsibilities

Brokers must provide accurate property information. Misleading or fraudulent listings will result in account termination and legal action.

4. Liability

Property Hub Uganda is not liable for any disputes between visitors and brokers. We provide a platform for connection – all transactions are between the visitor and the broker.

5. Reporting Fraud

If you suspect fraud, report the broker immediately using the "Report Broker" button on their profile. We will investigate and take appropriate action.

6. Account Termination

We reserve the right to terminate accounts that violate our terms of service or engage in fraudulent activity.
